On data, trust, and running

Good analysis knows what it doesn’t know.

Run Detective began with a simple idea: training data should help a runner understand a week, not just decorate it with charts.

The app reads workouts, heart-rate samples, and routes from Apple Health. It brings them into a view of recent runs, rolling totals, weekly comparisons, trends, and route details. The calculations are deterministic and documented, so the numbers have a path back to the observations that produced them.

Missing data should stay missing.

That principle shapes the product. Heart-rate analysis needs enough sample coverage. An in-progress week is compared with the equivalent portion of another week. When there are no runs, the interface says there is insufficient data. Routes stay on the device, and the project does not send workout data to external analytics or accounts.

The current release is an early MVP. That is part of the story too: it shows what is implemented, what has been tested on devices, and which ideas still need work.

On state, sync, and small details

A simple tracker becomes a systems problem.

Dony’s Lifts is a workout log built around a weekly routine: exercises, sets, targets, notes, and the small satisfaction of marking progress.

The interesting work starts when that routine moves between devices. The native app spans iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac. A shared record keeps the schedule available across platforms. The watch can queue changes while offline and pass them through its paired iPhone.

Synchronization is a product decision, not just a network call.

If two devices edit the record at once, the app pauses shared saving and asks which copy to keep. Local copies and backups support recovery. Apple Fitness activity stays on the iPhone rather than being added to the shared record. Those choices are less visible than an animation, but they are what make a personal tool feel dependable.

The repository documents current behavior, setup, platform differences, and limits. I care about that kind of clarity as much as the finished interface.
